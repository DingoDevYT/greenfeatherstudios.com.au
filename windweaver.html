Grounded, But Not Giving Up

Jonathan can't fly anymore, so he learned to run. Windweaver is a high-octane, boost-style 3D platformer about ripping through breathtaking landscapes at breakneck speed, riding the wind, and never, ever losing your momentum.

As you travel, you'll start to discover the truth of this world, and that everything is not as it was told to you.

Speed Is Everything

Read the rhythm of the sloped terrain to hold your top speed,
then shatter your limits with a deep, expressive movement toolkit.

Build Your Perfect Playstyle

Equip Pins to completely reshape how Jonathan moves, then swing by the Blacksmith to upgrade them. A few of them:

  • Slipstream

    The longer you dash without stopping, the faster you go. Break your dash and the bonus resets.

  • Bounce

    Landing a Dive launches you straight back into the air. Chain Dives back-to-back.

  • Aircutter

    Your Dive angles sharply downward instead of straight down, covering horizontal distance fast.

  • Charge Slam

    Hold Dive to charge up. A full charge hits 60% faster and damages everything nearby on landing.

  • Showoff

    Jump while airborne to pull off a trick. Each one refills some dash energy and powers up your next Fall Charge.

  • Glass Cannon

    You move 10% faster, but a single hit kills you instantly. Don't get hit.

  • Greedy

    Each seed you collect restores 10% of your dash energy. Dash, collect, repeat.

  • Hangry

    The more seeds you've collected this run, the faster you move. Keep collecting to reach top speed.

Plus the Common set, and three tiers of upgrades on every one of them.
